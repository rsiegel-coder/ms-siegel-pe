Our Mission

Movement with purpose. Growth with confidence.

Why We Do PE

Our goal is bigger than learning sports skills. Students work toward stronger fitness, healthy habits, confidence, responsibility, and the ability to stay active throughout life.

Every student is an athlete in this class.

Being an athlete here does not mean being the fastest or strongest. It means showing up, working, growing, and becoming the best version of yourself.

Ms. Siegel's Teaching Mission

I teach to equip students with the tools they need to succeed in school and beyond. My goal is to help them become confident, responsible individuals, critical thinkers, and active, engaged citizens.

Our daily goal: To be better than yesterday.

Healthy Habits Beyond PE

Small choices build lifelong habits.

🏃
Move regularly

Movement supports strength, endurance, confidence and well-being.

💧
Hydrate

Bring a reusable water bottle and drink throughout the day.

👟
Dress for movement

Comfortable shoes make activity safer and more enjoyable.

☀️
Protect yourself outdoors

A hat and sunscreen are smart choices on sunny days.

⭐
Bring a good attitude

Effort and attitude are things you control.

🤝
Respect the team

How you treat other people always matters.

👟 PE Clothing

There is no PE uniform. Students usually participate in their regular school clothes. They are welcome to bring an extra shirt or preferred running shoes, and running shoes may be left in the classroom if helpful.

❤️ Families are part of the team.

Encouragement at home makes a difference. Celebrate effort, participation, perseverance and growth—not just a time or score.
